\section{2021 年英语一真题阅读}
\subsection{Text 1 — The Value of Higher Education}

\begin{Paragraph}[reset]

    \Sentence{How does the author view the value of higher education in the
    modern economy?}

    \Sentence{The financial return on a college degree has become a
    widely debated topic, with some arguing that the rising cost of tuition
    has diminished the economic benefits.}

    \Sentence{However, recent research suggests that the wage premium
    for college graduates remains substantial, particularly in fields
    requiring specialized technical skills.}

    \Sentence{Moreover, the non-monetary benefits of education — including
    better health outcomes, greater civic engagement, and higher life
    satisfaction — are often overlooked in cost-benefit analyses.}
    \begin{translation}
        高等教育的非货币收益——包括更好的健康状况、更高的公民参与度
        以及更高的生活满意度——在成本效益分析中常常被忽视。
    \end{translation}

\end{Paragraph}

\begin{Paragraph}[reset]

    \Sentence{What is the main concern raised about the cost of college?}

    \Sentence{The primary concern is that tuition fees have grown at a rate
    far exceeding inflation, placing an increasingly heavy burden on
    middle-class families who do not qualify for substantial financial aid.}

    \Sentence{This has led to a situation where students from disadvantaged
    backgrounds are either priced out of higher education entirely or forced
    to take on significant debt that constrains their career choices.}
    \begin{translation}
        这导致了一种情况：来自弱势背景的学生要么完全被高等教育
        的高昂费用拒之门外，要么被迫承担巨额债务，从而限制了他们的职业选择。
    \end{translation}

    \Sentence{Policymakers have proposed various solutions, ranging from
    income-driven repayment plans to tuition-free community college
    programs, but consensus remains elusive.}

\end{Paragraph}

\section{2021 年英语一真题阅读}
\subsection{Text 2 — Climate Policy Challenges}

\begin{Paragraph}[reset]

    \Sentence{The implementation of effective climate policies faces
    numerous obstacles at both the national and international levels.}

    \Sentence{While the scientific consensus on anthropogenic climate
    change is overwhelming, translating this consensus into actionable
    policy has proven remarkably difficult.}

    \Sentence{One key challenge is the temporal mismatch between the
    immediate costs of climate action and the long-term benefits,
    which creates political incentives for inaction.}

    \Sentence{Furthermore, the unequal distribution of climate impacts
    across different regions and socioeconomic groups complicates
    efforts to build broad-based political coalitions.}
    \begin{translation}
        此外，气候变化对不同地区和社会经济群体的影响分布不均，
        这使得建立广泛政治联盟的努力变得更加复杂。
    \end{translation}

\end{Paragraph}
